<span style="color: #8B0000;">**UPC Labor PDI Works Council Elections (2026)**</span>

<span style="color: #8B0000;">**_— For a university for the working class!_**</span>

*Colleagues!*

The <span style="color: #8B0000;">**CGT union branch at UPC **</span> ens
is running in the <span style="color: #8B0000;">**Labor PDI Works Council elections**</span> to transform the rules of the game from the bottom up, far from bureaucratic and compromise-oriented unionism.

Please find attached our <span style="color: #8B0000;">**programa electoral**</span>, summarized in three core pillars:

<span style="color: #8B0000;">★</span> <span style="color: #8B0000;">**Class-conscious, assembly-based, and combative unionism**</span>, Workers' assemblies as the ultimate decision-making body. We prioritize direct action, full transparency, complaints to the Labor Inspectorate, and strikes over closed-door bargaining.

<span style="color: #8B0000;">★</span> <span style="color: #8B0000;">**No more precariousness for all PDI:**</span> immediate regularisation of structural associate professors and maximum wage parity; dignified conditions for substitute teachers (no more cheap labor); full implementation of the EPIF statute for predocs (guaranteed 4th year and teaching limits); and stabilization without punitive evaluations for assistant professors (lectoras).

<span style="color: #8B0000;">★</span> <span style="color: #8B0000;">**Decent living conditions:**</span> Faced with a 20% loss in purchasing power since 2010 and the runaway surge in rents, we are linking our wage demands—rejecting pay increases that fall short of the actual CPI—to the struggle for the right to decent housing. The agreement reached between the government and certain trade unions (an 11% raise spread over five years) is already completely inadequate, even before its full implementation.

![Bretxa salarial](/assets/img/Bretxa.png)

<span style="color: #8B0000;">**Bursting the university bubble:**</span> we demand UPC sever all institutional, academic, and business ties with the State of Israel against the genocide in Palestine, link our wage demands to the struggle for decent housing, and champion class-conscious feminism against harassment and abuses of power.

<span style="color: #8B0000;">***NWe are not here to manage our misery, but to organize and fight!***</span>

<span style="color: #8B0000;">***Vote for the CGT candidacy!***</span>
